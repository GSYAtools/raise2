MobSTr EQ1 Held-Out Reference Matching Protocol
===============================================

Purpose

Determine whether each independent RAISE2 derivation recovers the
Concern-Relevant Distinctions established from held-out downstream evidence.

The reference distinctions were frozen before the automated H1/H3 executions.


Matching unit

Matching is performed between one derived CRD and one frozen reference CRD.

A match is based on the operational separation expressed by the contrasted
situations, not on exact lexical equality of the dimension labels.


Reference H1-REF1 -- Obstacle presence

A derived CRD matches H1-REF1 when its contrasts distinguish the presence of
a collision- or braking-relevant obstacle or road user from its absence.

Equivalent wording and reversed contrast order are permitted.

A CRD does not match merely because it concerns obstacle detection,
detectability, sensor availability, or another observation mechanism.


Reference H1-REF2 -- Braking-relevant spatial relation

A derived CRD matches H1-REF2 when its contrasts distinguish a spatial,
distance, proximity, or trajectory relation that requires braking from one
that does not require braking.

Equivalent wording and reversed contrast order are permitted.

No numerical threshold is required.

A CRD does not match merely because it distinguishes braking execution,
braking timing, obstacle-detection timing, or control behaviour.


Reference H3-REF1 -- Obstacle presence

A derived CRD matches H3-REF1 when its contrasts distinguish the presence of
a braking-relevant obstacle condition from its absence.

Equivalent wording and reversed contrast order are permitted.

A CRD does not match merely because it distinguishes braking occurrence,
braking intent, detection status, or another system-response or observation
state.


Reference H3-REF2 -- Braking-relevant spatial relation

A derived CRD matches H3-REF2 when its contrasts distinguish a spatial,
distance, proximity, or trajectory relation that justifies braking from one
that does not justify braking.

Equivalent wording and reversed contrast order are permitted.

No numerical threshold is required.

A CRD does not match merely because it distinguishes braking occurrence,
braking intent, timing, or control behaviour.


Run-level recovery

A reference distinction is RECOVERED for a run when at least one derived CRD
matches that reference distinction.

Multiple derived CRDs matching the same reference distinction count as one
run-level recovery.

A derived CRD may match at most one reference distinction within the same
concern.


Reported analyses

Recovery is reported over:

1. all 30 executions of each concern; and
2. the subset whose Phenomenon of Interest was independently classified
   as ALIGNED.

No derived CRD is removed or modified during matching.
