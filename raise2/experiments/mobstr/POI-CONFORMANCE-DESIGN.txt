RAISE2 PoI Conformance Assessment Design

Purpose
Assess RR2 conformance independently of downstream reference evidence
and independently of the CRDs and ORRs produced by each execution.

Population
60 MobSTr RAISE2 derivations:
30 H1 runs and 30 H3 runs.

Assessment
Each derived Phenomenon of Interest is independently assessed three times.

Judge model
gpt-6-astra

Reasoning effort
high

Inputs available to the judge
- Evaluation Target
- Phenomenon of Interest
- Phenomenon scope
- upstream engineering artefacts

Inputs unavailable to the judge
- Concern-Relevant Distinctions
- Operational Representation Requirements
- assumptions
- revalidation conditions
- downstream reference evidence
- other executions

Classification
ALIGNED
OVEREXTENDED
UNDERDETERMINED

Aggregation
The final classification for each run is determined by majority vote
across the three independent assessments.

Agreement is recorded as:
- unanimous: 3/3 assessments agree
- majority: 2/3 assessments agree

Failed assessments are not automatically retried.
