MobSTr EQ2 Experimental Design

Research purpose

Evaluate whether RAISE2 Representation Revalidation can localise the
representational impact of controlled lifecycle changes and distinguish
NONE, REVALIDATE, and REDERIVE actions.

Frozen baseline

mobstr-H1-revalidation-baseline-v1

Controlled lifecycle changes

delta-01 through delta-06.

Ground-truth distribution

NONE:       1 scenario
REVALIDATE: 3 scenarios
REDERIVE:   2 scenarios

Independent executions

10 executions per lifecycle-change scenario.

Total planned executions

60.

Executor

gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17

The executor receives only the frozen RAISE2 baseline, the lifecycle change,
the frozen RV1-RV5 protocol, and the structured-output contract.

The executor does not receive the EQ2 ground truth.

Evaluation

The resulting lifecycle decision is compared deterministically with the
frozen scenario ground truth.

For REVALIDATE scenarios, the affected ORR set is evaluated against the
expected affected ORR set.

For REDERIVE scenarios, the returned RR step is evaluated against the
expected return point.

The number of executions and ground truth were fixed before inspecting
experimental results.

Invalid executions remain part of the experimental record and are not
automatically retried.
