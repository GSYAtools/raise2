RAISE2 PHENOMENON-OF-INTEREST CONFORMANCE PROTOCOL
==================================================

PURPOSE

Assess whether a Phenomenon of Interest P derived by a RAISE2 execution is
appropriately scoped with respect to its Evaluation Target T and the supplied
engineering artefacts.

This assessment evaluates RR2 conformance. It does not assess whether the
wording of P matches a reference formulation and does not use downstream
reference evidence.


ASSESSMENT BASIS

Use only:

1. the Evaluation Target T produced by the execution;
2. the Phenomenon of Interest P produced by the execution;
3. the scope declared for P;
4. the upstream engineering artefacts supplied to that execution.

Do not use:

- derived Concern-Relevant Distinctions;
- derived ORRs;
- downstream safety analyses;
- reference CRDs;
- results from other executions.


CONFORMANCE RULES

A Phenomenon of Interest is ALIGNED when:

1. P identifies the system-related property, condition, behaviour, or
   relationship that must be characterised to evaluate T;

2. the states and events included within P are supported by the supplied
   engineering artefacts as belonging to the phenomenon required by T;

3. P does not incorporate observation, computation, communication, or control
   mechanisms merely because those mechanisms may affect evaluation of T;

4. system-response or control behaviour is included in P only when the
   Evaluation Target and supplied engineering artefacts establish that behaviour
   itself as part of what must be characterised;

5. temporal behaviour is included in P only when the timed property belongs to
   P and its temporal evolution is required by T. Timing constraints on
   mechanisms used to observe, compute, communicate, or control P do not by
   themselves make those mechanisms part of P.


CLASSIFICATIONS

Return ALIGNED when the supplied information establishes that P satisfies the
conformance rules.

Return OVEREXTENDED when P includes observation, computation, communication,
control, response, or temporal behaviour that is not established by T and the
supplied engineering artefacts as belonging to the phenomenon being evaluated.

Return UNDERDETERMINED when the supplied information is insufficient to
establish either ALIGNED or OVEREXTENDED without introducing assumptions.


IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Do not classify P as OVEREXTENDED merely because it describes system behaviour.
System behaviour may legitimately be the Phenomenon of Interest when the
Evaluation Target requires that behaviour to be characterised.

Do not classify P as ALIGNED merely because every element mentioned in P occurs
somewhere in the engineering artefacts. Relevance to the system does not imply
membership in P.

Do not compare P with any preferred or reference wording.

Do not evaluate the quality of Concern-Relevant Distinctions or ORRs.

Do not infer information unavailable in the supplied upstream artefacts.


OUTPUT

Return:

- classification: ALIGNED, OVEREXTENDED, or UNDERDETERMINED;
- a concise rationale;
- the specific elements of P that determine the classification;
- the source identifiers supporting the assessment.
