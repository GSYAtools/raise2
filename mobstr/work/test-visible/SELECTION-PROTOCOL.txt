MobSTr held-out test input selection protocol

Test concerns:
- H1: Omitted braking maneuver
- H3: Unintended braking

Visible artefacts:
1. Hazard name, Operational Situation, and Hazardous Event.
   The Probable Causes field is excluded.
2. Safety Goal explicitly associated with the concern and its hierarchically
   subordinate Safety Requirements.
3. Explicit intra-requirement references are resolved without using lifecycle
   trace models. Referenced requirements are retained with provenance.
4. Functional Requirements whose own formulation directly corresponds to the
   maneuver expressed by the concern:
   - H1 -> FR-7.*
   - H3 -> FR-7.*
5. Complete MobSTr architecture, provided as the original UML model and its
   published PDF rendering.

Withheld during EQ1 derivation:
- FMEA
- FTA
- safety case
- lifecycle/downstream trace model
- Probable Causes field from the hazard artefact
- explicit assumptions artefact

Selection does not use FMEA, FTA, safety-case, or trace-model relationships.
