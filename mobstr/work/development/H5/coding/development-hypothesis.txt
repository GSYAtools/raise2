Development hypothesis after H2 and H5 RR1--RR2:

A requirement associated with an evaluative concern does not automatically
define a Concern-Relevant Distinction.

A candidate distinction is admissible only if:
1. both contrasted situations belong to the declared Phenomenon of Interest;
2. collapsing them removes information required to evaluate the concern.

Requirements constraining observation, computation, communication, or control
mechanisms may instead establish dependencies, assumptions, revalidation
conditions, or preservation threats.

This hypothesis remains provisional until H5 RR3--RR5 and held-reference
analysis are completed.
