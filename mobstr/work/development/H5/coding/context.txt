CONCERN: H5 -- Unintended leaving of the road

RR1 -- Evaluative Intent:
Determine whether the vehicle's operational motion remains consistent with
the intended trajectory and road boundaries.

Source rationale:
- SG-5 requires prevention of unintended leaving of the road.
- H5 describes the vehicle following a wrong trajectory and leaving the road.
- FR-6 requires the vehicle to define and follow a trajectory through
  predetermined way points.
- FR-6.5--FR-6.10 constrain steering, speed, vehicle state, and localization
  relevant to maintaining that trajectory.

RR2 -- Phenomenon of Interest:
The vehicle's trajectory-relative operational motion with respect to the
road boundaries.

Phenomenon scope:
The phenomenon includes the vehicle's actual position and motion, its
relationship to the intended trajectory and road boundaries, and the
longitudinal and lateral evolution of that relationship.

Representation/control boundary:
Sensor outputs, localization estimates, path-planner states, controller
states, and control-command generation mechanisms are not themselves treated
as the Phenomenon of Interest. Requirements on these mechanisms support a CRD
only when they establish a concern-relevant difference between operational
situations within the declared phenomenon.

PoI scope-consistency check:
Candidate CRDs must contrast two situations belonging to the vehicle's
trajectory-relative operational motion. A requirement relevant to H5 does not
automatically define a CRD when it constrains only the mechanism used to
observe, compute, communicate, or control that phenomenon.

Possible additional PoIs:
None introduced at this stage. A single PoI is retained unless RR3 identifies
a source-supported concern-relevant distinction that cannot be expressed as a
difference within the declared phenomenon.
