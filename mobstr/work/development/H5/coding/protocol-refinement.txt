Provisional protocol refinement from H2/H5 development

CP3 -- Dimension admissibility
Admit an evaluation dimension only when:
(1) the contrasted situations are states of the Phenomenon of Interest
    established in RR2; and
(2) variation along that dimension can be traced to visible engineering
    artefacts as potentially changing the judgement expressed by C.

A requirement constraining an observation, computation, communication, or
control mechanism does not by itself establish a CRD of P, even when violation
of that requirement may affect the evaluation.

CP12 -- Temporal scope
A temporal contrast is admissible only when the timed property belongs to P
and its temporal evolution is required by C. Timing constraints on mechanisms
used to observe, compute, communicate, or control P are treated as dependencies
or preservation conditions unless the source artefacts establish the timed
behaviour itself as part of the phenomenon being evaluated.

Development implication for H2
The initially derived H2 temporal CRDs must be re-examined under these refined
rules before the development protocol is frozen. The original pre-reference
H2 derivation remains preserved as the development record.
