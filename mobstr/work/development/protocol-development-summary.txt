Development summary after H2 and H5

Main refinement:
A requirement relevant to an evaluative concern does not automatically define
a Concern-Relevant Distinction.

Refined CP3 -- Dimension admissibility:
Admit an evaluation dimension only when:
(1) the contrasted situations are states of the Phenomenon of Interest
    established in RR2; and
(2) variation along that dimension is supported by visible engineering
    artefacts as potentially changing the judgement expressed by C.
Requirements constraining observation, computation, communication, or control
mechanisms do not by themselves establish CRDs of P.

Refined CP12 -- Temporal scope:
A temporal contrast is admissible only when the timed property belongs to P
and its temporal evolution is required by C. Timing constraints on mechanisms
used to observe, compute, communicate, or control P are instead treated as
dependencies, assumptions, revalidation conditions, or preservation threats
unless the source establishes the timed behaviour itself as part of P.

Reference interpretation:
Downstream evidence is classified as:
(a) phenomenon-level distinction evidence;
(b) preservation/dependency threat;
(c) execution/control failure;
before matching against derived CRDs.
