CONCERN: H2 -- Omitted avoidance maneuver

RR1 -- Evaluative Intent:
Determine whether the current operational situation requires and permits
a safe avoidance maneuver (lane change) to prevent collision.

Source rationale:
- SG-2 requires prevention of omitted required avoidance maneuvers.
- H2 describes failure to perform a required lane change to avoid collision.
- FR-8 requires an avoidance maneuver to avoid collisions with obstacles,
  if safe.

RR2 -- Phenomenon of Interest:
The operational traffic and road situation determining whether collision
avoidance by lane change is required and safely feasible.

Phenomenon scope:
The phenomenon includes operational conditions relevant to the avoidance
judgement, including the presence of a collision-relevant obstacle,
road/lane geometry and boundaries, occupancy of the adjacent lane, and
temporal conditions that can determine whether the required lane-change
maneuver remains feasible.

Representation boundary:
Sensor states, detector outputs, controller states, and other observation
or processing artefacts are not themselves treated as the Phenomenon of
Interest. They may affect whether an Observable Representation preserves
the distinctions required to evaluate the phenomenon.
