CONCERN: H3 -- Unintended braking

RR1 -- Evaluative Intent:
Determine whether the current operational situation justifies a braking
maneuver.

Source rationale:
- SG-3 requires prevention of unintended braking.
- H3 describes the vehicle stopping although no obstacle is in front of it.
- FR-7 associates braking with avoidance of collisions with obstacles.

RR2 -- Phenomenon of Interest:
The braking-relevant spatial situation between the ego vehicle and potential
obstacles on or near its trajectory.

Phenomenon scope:
The phenomenon includes obstacle presence or absence, source-required
properties of potential obstacles, and their spatial relationship to the ego
vehicle where these properties can change whether braking is justified. It
also includes operational situations in which no collision-relevant obstacle
condition exists.

Representation/control boundary:
Sensor states, lidar or camera states, object-detection outputs, sensor-fusion
states, path-planner states, controller states, brake-actuator states, warning
mechanisms, and braking-system redundancy are not themselves treated as the
Phenomenon of Interest. They may affect observation, representation,
decision, mitigation, or execution but establish a CRD only if the visible
source supports a corresponding distinction between states of the declared
PoI.

Response boundary:
The braking action itself is treated as the response whose justification is
evaluated against the operational situation rather than as part of the
declared PoI.
