CONCERN: H1 -- Omitted braking maneuver

RR1 -- Evaluative Intent:
Determine whether the current operational situation requires a braking
maneuver to avoid collision.

Source rationale:
- SG-1 requires prevention of omitted required braking maneuvers.
- H1 describes failure to stop in time to avoid collision with a road user
  or static obstacle.
- FR-7 requires a braking maneuver to avoid collisions with obstacles.

RR2 -- Phenomenon of Interest:
The collision-relevant spatial situation between the ego vehicle and
obstacles on or near its trajectory.

Phenomenon scope:
The phenomenon includes concern-relevant properties of obstacles and their
spatial relationship to the ego vehicle, including obstacle presence,
source-required obstacle categories, and relative distance where these
properties can change whether braking is required.

Representation/control boundary:
Sensor states, lidar or camera states, object-detection outputs, sensor-fusion
states, path-planner states, controller states, and brake-actuator states are
not themselves treated as the Phenomenon of Interest. They may affect
observation, representation, decision, or execution but establish a CRD only
if the visible source supports a corresponding distinction between states of
the declared PoI.

Response boundary:
The braking action itself is treated as the response to the evaluated
operational situation rather than as part of the declared PoI.
