Development record

Development concerns:
- H2: Omitted avoidance maneuver
- H5: Unintended leaving of the road

Held-out test concerns:
- H1: Omitted braking maneuver
- H3: Unintended braking

The development phase was used to stabilise the operational coding protocol
before analysis of the held-out concerns.

Main refinement identified during development:
Requirements relevant to an evaluative concern do not automatically establish
Concern-Relevant Distinctions. Candidate contrasts must first satisfy
Phenomenon-of-Interest membership and the collapse test.

In particular, H2/H5 exposed that timing, observation, computation, and
control requirements may establish dependencies or preservation conditions
without defining independent CRDs of the declared PoI.

Downstream reference interpretation was consequently fixed to distinguish:
(1) phenomenon-level distinction evidence;
(2) preservation/dependency threats; and
(3) execution/control failures.

No H2 or H5 observation contributes to the held-out EQ1 effectiveness metrics.
